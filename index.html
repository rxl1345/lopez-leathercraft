Our Story
First Generation

From Oaxaca

In the mountains of southern Mexico, our grandfather fed his family with leather. Saddlery, belts, bags, small goods made for other businesses to sell. Nine children. One workshop. Every stitch put food on the table.

First Generation

Hands That Taught Hands

Our father grew up in that workshop. One of the oldest, he became his father's hands. He didn't just learn how to cut and stitch. He learned what it meant to make something that someone else would depend on.

Second Generation

By Way of Texas

When he moved to Texas, the craft came with him. Belts, wallets, knife holsters. Smaller than what his father built, but the intention was the same. Every piece was a conversation with Oaxaca.

Second Generation

The Reason Didn't Change

He wasn't feeding nine children with leather. He was holding onto his father, his roots, his identity. The workshop changed. The tools changed. The reason didn't.

Third Generation

Growing Up Watching

I grew up watching my father work. The smell of leather, the sound of a mallet on a stamp, the patience of a hand stitch. I was fascinated before I understood what any of it meant.

Third Generation

Father and Son, Side by Side

Today, my father and I work together on new designs. Belts, laptop sleeves, desk mats, knife sheaths, camera straps, wallets, and wet molded bags. Three generations of knowledge flow through every piece we make.
